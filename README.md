# 🎓 Sindh University Entry Test (PET) 2025–2026 Preparation Suite

An interactive, mobile-optimized mock exam and past paper solving suite for candidates appearing in the **University of Sindh Pre-Entry Test (PET)**.

🌐 **Live Web App:** [https://sarmad-mahar.github.io/sindh-uni-pet-prep/](https://sarmad-mahar.github.io/sindh-uni-pet-prep/)

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Features
- **10 Complete Papers (250 MCQs)**:
  - 7 Authentic Past Papers (2013, 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019, PET 2025 Mock)
  - 3 High-Yield Predictive Model Papers
- **Targeted Subjects**:
  - English (Vocabulary, Grammar, Synonyms, Antonyms, Sentence Correction)
  - General Knowledge (Sindh & Pakistan History, Geography, Islamiyat, World)
  - General Science (Everyday Biology, Chemistry, Physics fundamentals)
- **3 Practice & Exam Modes**:
  - **Practice Mode**: Real-time feedback with detailed explanations and rationales.
  - **Timed Exam Mode**: 25-minute exam countdown simulation without answer spoilers.
  - **Study Sheet Mode**: Instant answer key and explanations for quick revision.
- **Auto-Save & In-Progress Recovery**:
  - Solve questions anytime. If you close the browser, your answers and progress are automatically saved and restored when you return!
- **Scorecard & Performance Analytics**:
  - Pass / Merit eligibility badge
  - Subject-by-subject accuracy breakdown
  - Review only mistakes or all answers
- **100% Mobile & Offline Friendly**:
  - Installable via "Add to Home Screen" as a PWA.
  - Runs with zero server dependencies.
